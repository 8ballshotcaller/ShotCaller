How to play a Combo
  1. Identify the target ball — this is the ball you ultimately want in the pocket.
  2. Identify the combo ball — the first object ball struck by the cue ball.
  3. The target ball's route to the pocket determines the direction it must leave the collision.
  4. Use the displayed line as the aiming reference for the combo ball's contact with the target.
  5. Then aim the cue ball through the displayed ghost-ball point on the combo ball.
  6. Use appropriate speed and remember that throw, cloth conditions, spin and imperfect contact can change the real result.
